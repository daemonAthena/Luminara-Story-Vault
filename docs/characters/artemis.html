New girl, stand straight; keep your hands from the shake.

Artemis is the ace pilot of Gorgon Operations Koros in a squad with Ampersat and Belial - Bad Bitch in a Perfect Uniform.

Artemis is technically the same person as Zenia. She is Zenia without her memories.

Biography

Before GOK Service

Her background is a mystery. It is unknown when she was born. She is a blank slate.

GOK Tenure

wiping her brain a second time certainly didnt help but it was just procedure.

Her arrival at GOK is the first thing she remembers.

First Gorgon Kill

Artemis was the first pilot to slay a Gorgon in GOK history. For this she recieved immense praise, had several AARs written for the high command to study, and was honored by the council of Angels.

Court Martial

Personality

Artemis is stoic and self sacrificing. She gets anxious when confronted by the cold impersonal world she was thrust into in her earliest memories. A feeling inside her drives her to take each step forward. She feels like she has a greater purpose than even the Council has designed for her.

If you ask her, she will say her duty is to her comrades and her people with the conviction of an old soul.

Relationships

Metiz Sanchez

Metiz is her handler and they start out with a bit of a rocky relationship.

Ampersat

Ampersat is her squadmate, the initial relationship between them is defined by animosity and an unspoken attraction.

Ampersat’s relationship with Artemis hit a low over her jealousy for the first gorgon kill. Ampersat had trained her whole life to kill the gorgon that killed her mother but Artemis showed up and within two deployments she killed it.

Belial

Belial is her squadmate. Initialy Belial felt an irrational hatred towards Artemis.
